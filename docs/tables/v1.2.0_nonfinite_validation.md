\# Non-finite Result Validation



The robustness experiment evaluates the behavior of Monte Carlo DSL v1.2.0 when the simulated model produces non-finite results.



The evaluated model is:



\\\[

X \\sim U(-10,10)

\\]



\\\[

Y = \\sqrt{X}

\\]



Values generated for \\(X < 0\\) produce non-finite results and are handled using the runtime policy `discard\_and\_report`.



For the valid population:



\\\[

P(X \\ge 0) = 0.5

\\]



\\\[

E\[Y \\mid X \\ge 0] = \\frac{2}{3}\\sqrt{10} \\approx 2.108185

\\]



\\\[

Var(Y \\mid X \\ge 0) = \\frac{5}{9} \\approx 0.555556

\\]



| Seed | Valid results | Discarded results | Valid rate | Estimated mean | Mean error (MCSE) | Passed |

|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|:---:|

| 101 | 50071 | 49929 | 0.50071 | 2.113216 | 1.512 | Yes |

| 202 | 49785 | 50215 | 0.49785 | 2.104731 | 1.030 | Yes |

| 303 | 49951 | 50049 | 0.49951 | 2.107104 | 0.323 | Yes |

| 404 | 49900 | 50100 | 0.49900 | 2.104684 | 1.051 | Yes |

| 505 | 50009 | 49991 | 0.50009 | 2.110833 | 0.796 | Yes |



Across 500,000 candidate results, 249,716 were valid and 250,284 were discarded.



The mean valid rate across seeds was 0.499432. All 95% Wilson intervals for the valid rate contained the theoretical value 0.5.



All estimated means remained within three Monte Carlo standard errors of the theoretical conditional mean. Result accounting was exact for every run, and the configured `discard\_and\_report` policy was reported consistently.
